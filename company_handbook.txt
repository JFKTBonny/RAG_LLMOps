COMPANY HANDBOOK — TECHCORP 2024

ONBOARDING
New employees must complete onboarding within the first 3 days of joining.
Onboarding includes HR documentation, IT setup, security training, and team introduction.
Each new hire is assigned a buddy from their team for the first month.
All new hires receive a $500 equipment allowance for home office setup.

REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Remote work requests must be submitted via the HR portal every Monday.
Employees working remotely must be available during core hours of 10am to 3pm.
A stable internet connection of at least 50Mbps is required for remote work.

VACATION AND LEAVE
Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year.
Vacation requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance via HR portal.
Unused vacation days can be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days per year.
Sick leave is separate from vacation and allows up to 10 days per year.
Parental leave is 16 weeks fully paid for primary caregivers.

PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year in June and December.
Each review includes a self assessment and a manager evaluation.
Employees scoring above expectations receive a bonus of up to 15% of salary.
Performance improvement plans are initiated for employees below expectations.

ENGINEERING TOOLS AND PRACTICES
The engineering team uses GitHub for version control and Jira for task tracking.
All code must be reviewed by at least one senior engineer before merging.
The team follows two week sprint cycles with planning on Mondays.
Python and JavaScript are the primary programming languages used by the team.
All services must include unit tests with minimum 80% code coverage.

COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
The company provides health insurance covering medical, dental and vision.
A 401k plan is available with company matching up to 5% of salary.
Employees receive a $1000 annual learning and development budget.
The company provides a monthly transport allowance of $150.
Staff are eligible for stock options after completing one year of employment.

IT AND SECURITY
The IT department handles all software installation requests within 24 hours.
All employees must complete security training within the first week of joining.
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 90 days.
Any security incidents must be reported immediately to security@techcorp.com.
All company data must be stored on approved cloud platforms only.

WORKPLACE HEALTH AND SAFETY
Staff members must report any workplace injuries immediately to their supervisor.
The office has a designated first aid officer on each floor.
Emergency evacuation drills are conducted every 6 months.
Ergonomic assessments are available for all employees upon request.
